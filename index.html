Configuring Last Seen Privacy

Last seen reveals your activity patterns. "Last seen recently" means within three days. "Last seen within a week" extends to seven days. "Last seen within a month" goes to 30 days. Or hide it completely. Trade-off: hiding your last seen also hides others' timestamps. Telegram mod apk breaks this restriction, letting you hide yours while still seeing theirs.

Profile Photo Visibility

Profile photos identify you visually. Control who sees them with three tiers: everybody, contacts only, or nobody. Add exceptions for specific users. Show your photo to close friends but hide it from work colleagues. Change settings per relationship category. Your photo becomes visible only where appropriate.

Advanced Privacy Features

Read Receipts Control

Blue checkmarks tell senders you've read their messages. Useful for close relationships. Problematic for acquaintances who expect instant responses. Telegram lets you disable read receipts. Messages still arrive. You still read them. But senders don't see confirmation. Reduced pressure. Increased flexibility.

Online Status Hiding

The "online" indicator updates whenever you open Telegram. Even brief app checks broadcast availability. Disable online status to browse privately. Check messages without signaling presence. Review conversations without inviting new ones. Your messaging becomes invisible, known only to you.

💡 Privacy Balance
Privacy isn't isolation. It's control. Share openly with trusted contacts. Restrict access from others. Telegram mod apk allows per-contact settings, creating custom privacy zones for each relationship type.

Phone Number Privacy

Why Phone Number Matters

Your phone number links Telegram to other services. People search by number to find accounts. Old contacts discover you've joined. Strangers from groups access your number. Default settings make numbers visible to anyone who shares a chat with you. Change this immediately.

Number Visibility Options

  • Nobody sees your number unless you share it manually
  • Only contacts you've saved see your number
  • Everyone in shared chats can see it (default, risky)
  • Custom exceptions for specific users or groups
  • Link to username instead for public sharing

Username as Alternative

Create a username for public identification. Share @yourusername instead of your number. People find you without accessing your phone number. Change usernames anytime. Disconnect from old identities easily. Maintain privacy while staying reachable.

Group and Channel Privacy

Group Member Visibility

Joining groups exposes you to all members. They see your profile photo, name, and potentially phone number depending on settings. Before joining large groups, verify your privacy settings protect sensitive information. Consider creating group-specific visibility rules.

Channel Subscription Privacy

Channel subscriptions remain private by default. Admins can't see subscriber lists. Your channel interests stay confidential. Exception: if you comment publicly, your name becomes visible to other readers. Telegram mod apk offers anonymous commenting in supported channels.

🛡️ Group Privacy Strategy
Create separate accounts for different social circles. Personal account for friends. Professional account for work groups. Anonymous account for public communities. Each maintains appropriate privacy boundaries.

Call and Video Privacy

Call Privacy Settings

Voice and video calls reveal your IP address to peer-to-peer connections. Enable "Use Proxy for Calls" to route calls through Telegram servers instead. Slight quality reduction. Significant privacy gain. Your actual location stays hidden from call participants.

Call Notification Control

  • Allow calls from everybody (default, convenient but public)
  • Restrict calls to contacts only
  • Block all calls entirely and use messages only
  • Add exceptions for specific users regardless of general setting

Screen Sharing Caution

Screen sharing during video calls exposes everything visible on your screen. Notifications pop up. Open apps become visible. Background images reveal personal information. Before sharing, close sensitive windows. Pause notifications. Switch to a clean desktop. Privacy requires vigilance.

Message Privacy Options

Forwarding Restrictions

Forwarded messages include attribution showing original sender. Disable forwarding for specific chats to prevent sharing. Useful for private groups where information should stay contained. Messages sent remain readable but can't be forwarded elsewhere, protecting confidential discussions.

Screenshot Notifications

Secret chats notify you when someone screenshots the conversation. Regular chats lack this protection. Assume anything in standard chats can be captured and shared. Use secret chats for truly sensitive information. They self-destruct and alert to screenshot attempts.
